Case 1 — Fixed-price automation — scope realignment & margin defense

Project Manager Fixed-price delivery 55% margin preserved

the friction

Client expected self-learning ML while the signed SOW specified deterministic automation. Edge-case omissions from discovery surfaced at the same time in front of executive sponsors, threatening project profitability.

the intervention

Reconciled discovery and contract baselines internally; absorbed minor discovery misses (~16 hrs) at $0 cost to preserve trust; held a firm line on ML by showing it was technically premature without prior data standardization.

the outcome

Defended 55% baseline margin; negotiated data standardization and ML modeling into a separate, paid 3-month contract extension; established upfront pre-sales AI/automation definition gates.

Case 2 — Platform migration (SAFe) — rapid team scaling, 15 → 25 engineers

Dedicated PM Global Oil & Gas client 10 engineers onboarded

the friction

Mid-program, an enterprise migration hit a budget-to-scope deficit. The deadline was at risk, and delivery was mathematically unfeasible with the existing 15-person team.

the intervention

Re-baselined scope with the Solution Architect; presented 3 trade-off scenarios to leadership; hired and onboarded 10 engineers/QAs in under a month; divided delivery into dual autonomous pods with a 3-week shadow buffer.

the outcome

Met the hard client deadline; shipped 100% of high-priority scope with planned variance; secured a follow-on Stage 2 contract extension.

retrospective lesson

The 3-week onboarding buffer strained senior tech leads balancing mentoring with delivery. Future rapid scale-ups require a 4-to-6 week staggered onboarding window.

Case 3 — Enterprise SaaS — client de-escalation & account recovery

Engagement Manager Enterprise system review 100% adoption secured

the friction

An enterprise client rejected post-implementation system recommendations as abstract and impractical. The client went silent and stopped responding to check-ins, halting delivery and putting account retention at risk.

the intervention

Intercepted the escalation directly with the client PM; partnered with consulting leadership to rotate the lead consultant for an SME with business-first advisory skills; re-ran discovery translating technical configurations into direct business ROI.

the outcome

Customer accepted and adopted 100% of the re-evaluated recommendations, and CSAT standing was preserved without commercial fallout or churn.

retrospective lesson

Mid-project feedback check-ins had gone unanswered earlier. Client silence is an active risk indicator requiring an immediate live touchpoint, not another email follow-up.

Risk management

Quantitative 5×5 severity matrix & active impediment resolution log

Risk vs. issue

A risk is a future event that could affect schedule or cost. An issue already exists and needs immediate handling.

Scoring

Likelihood (1–5) × Impact (1–5) = Severity (1–25), so prioritization is a calculation, not a judgment call.

Mitigation strategy

Every risk is assigned Accept, Mitigate, Transfer, or Avoid at the point of entry — not after the fact.

Early warning

Leading symptoms are logged against each risk, so a trigger prompts action before it becomes an issue.
